About the club

Riverside Rowing Club was founded in 1908 by a group of dock workers who wanted to race on their day off. More than a century later the club is still entirely member run: every coach, boat officer and tea maker is a volunteer.

Our facilities

  • Two boat bays holding 14 eights, 9 fours and 20 singles
  • A 12-station erg room with mirrors and a projector for technique work
  • Changing rooms with hot showers (a club tradition)
  • A club room that seats 60 for suppers and talks

How to join

Turn up to a taster session, complete a capsize drill and a swim test, then choose between the novice or masters squad. Membership fees are £240 a year, with a concession rate for students.
