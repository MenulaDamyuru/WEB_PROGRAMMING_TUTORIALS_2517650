Welcome to the club

We are a community club with around 180 members, from complete beginners to masters athletes. Our boathouse sits on the Midsummer Common reach, a short walk from the city centre, and we row all year round.

New members are always welcome. Our learn-to-row courses run in April and September and no previous experience is needed — just a willingness to get wet in your first outing.

Come and try it

Taster sessions run every Saturday at 10:00 during term time. Bring trainers, a water bottle and clothes you don't mind getting damp. Everything else is provided.
